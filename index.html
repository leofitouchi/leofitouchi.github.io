Léo Fitouchi

Marie Skłodowska-Curie
Postdoctoral Fellow

MIT and Harvard University

I'm a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at MIT (Saxe Lab) and Harvard (Cushman Lab). I study the cognitive architecture of moral judgment: What are the mental algorithms that produce our intuitions of right and wrong? How do these cognitive algorithms, in turn, shape universal features of human culture, such as moral norms, religious traditions, and punitive institutions?

I tackle these questions by integrating insights from cognitive science, evolutionary theory, and cross-cultural anthropology; I test the resulting theories with psychological experiments, computational modeling, and cross-cultural databases.
