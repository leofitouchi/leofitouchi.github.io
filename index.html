Léo Fitouchi

Marie Curie Postdoctoral Fellow

MIT, Harvard University

I'm a postdoctoral fellow, currently joining the labs of Rebecca Saxe at MIT and Fiery Cushman at Harvard. I'm funded by a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Global Fellowship on the cognitive foundations of legitimate authority.

I study the cognitive architecture of moral judgment. What are the mental algorithms that produce our intuitions of right and wrong? How do these cognitive mechanisms shape normative phenomena that recur across cultures—such as moral norms, religious traditions, and punitive institutions?

To answer these questions, I integrate insights from cognitive science, evolutionary theory, and cross-cultural anthropology, and test the resulting theories with psychological experiments, computational modeling, and cross-cultural databases.
